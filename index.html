The Syndicate

Art that remembers where it came from — and speaks to wherever it is going.

Christarman Art Syndicate is a multidisciplinary studio working across painting, charcoal, portraiture and sculpture. Each piece is made by hand, drawing on West African visual heritage — the gele and the talking drum, the stilt village and the water bearer — and carrying it into the contemporary collection.

From intimate commissioned portraits to monumental sculpture, every work is created to be lived with for generations.

    The Studio

    Made by hand. Made to last.

    Working from the studio, Christarman moves freely between canvas, paper, clay and cast form. Charcoal portraits are built layer by layer; paintings range from luminous pointillism to bold abstraction; sculptures are modelled, cast and finished by hand.

    Each original is signed by the artist. Collectors receive the work with care instructions and documentation of authenticity.
